# Revision 3 — explicit decisions, budget accounting and resumable main cohort

Revision 2 is complete and remains unchanged: ten terminal pilot records, two
explicit accepted artifacts, and eight missing decisions. Its six budget
exhaustions, three timeouts and two transport errors overlap. B0 passed both
pilot inputs. These results qualify the execution path, not a superiority claim.

The new main revision preserves the five arms, twelve acquisitions, three
repeats, balanced order, MIDAS release, requested model, available documents,
1,800-second limit, 40-request limit and 16,000-token per-response ceiling.
The budget is unchanged: a budget failure is an outcome of this resource-limited
comparison. No unsuccessful pilot is selectively rerun.

Changes before collecting the 180 main trials:

- Require a valid explicit final decision manifest. The historical rig's
  single-geometry fallback remains available only outside this study.
- Keep request-budget exhaustion in the scientific denominator; retain
  transport/identity/usage issues separately. Preserve inner exit-124 timeouts.
  Artifacts produced during failed executions remain visible but cannot turn
  a failed execution into a completed trial.
- Observe automatic procedure blocks without changing their contents and
  record exact-block hashes and roles when they occur in outgoing requests.
  The prepared document text is preserved separately from private profiles.
  Fresh file-copy qualification must demonstrate delivery to A1 and none to
  A7. Explicit document access remains available to both.
- Report seeder identity, template phase and exported effective phase separately.
  A single calibration `--calibrant` selects the seeder; it does not replace
  the template's fitted phase. Numerical semantics are unchanged.
- Admit main execution only after a hash-bound review of the retained pilot,
  fixed baseline, regression checks and new startup qualification.
- Use one immutable schedule and durable start/terminal records. Resuming
  skips completed trials; a previously started trial lacking a terminal record
  stays interrupted, without automatic repetition. Every scheduled trial remains
  visible. Main B0 runs once per acquisition and is separately resumable.

All numerical trials share the existing scientific-run lock. The gold FF case
study uses a separate protocol and results tree. Do not interpret its task or
resource use as part of the native-system comparison. Main comparative claims
remain pending the complete cohort and effective-artifact review.

The raw calibration reference is a fitted leave-one-out trajectory, not absolute
metrology. Report all-started completion alongside technically evaluable results;
repeated model trials do not create independent physical acquisitions.
